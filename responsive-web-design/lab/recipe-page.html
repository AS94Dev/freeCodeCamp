Pancakes

Welcome to the ultimate guide for making fluffy pancakes! These classic treats are perfect for satisfying your sweet tooth without overindulging. Follow this simple recipe to create delicious, crispy-on-the-outside, fluffy-on-the-inside pancakes that everyone will love.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1/4 cup unsalted butter, melted
  • 1/4 cup granulated sugar
  • 1/2 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1/2 cup milk

Instructions

  1. Preheat your non-stick skillet or griddle over medium heat and lightly grease it with butter.
  2. In a bowl, whisk together the flour and baking powder.
  3. In another bowl, beat the melted butter, sugar, and vanilla extract until combined.
  4. Gradually add the dry ingredients to the wet mixture, then pour in the milk and mix until smooth.
  5. Drop small spoonfuls of batter onto the hot skillet.
  6. Cook for 2-3 minutes until bubbles form on the surface, then flip and cook the other side until golden brown before enjoying!
